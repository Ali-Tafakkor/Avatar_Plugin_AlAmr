For full-body 3D web-avatar work, read avatar-threejs-builder/SKILL.md version 1.3.0. For a new identity, construct fresh neutral facial geometry from the user's references, starting with empty authoring space or non-facial primitives. Do not inherit a finished face, stock/parametric identity template, donor landmark cage or donor morph deltas. Compare neutral clay front/profile/three-quarter geometry without textures, hair, beard or glasses before polish. Author topology, eyes/lids, mouth interior, UVs, skinning and expression/speech deformers for that anatomy. Reuse semantic channel meanings, controller timing and compatible body/hand behavior, with new mappings and calibration. Ask about unclear clothing. Report unknown reference views and actual authoring blockers; never silently replace the new head with a donor. An explicit existing-character repair or integration of a user-supplied finished target preserves that identity within scope. Deliver editable geometry provenance, actual likeness and behavior evidence, source and tested assets. Numerical utility tests alone do not certify likeness.
